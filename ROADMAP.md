# Roadmap

## v0.5 candidates

- Community-reviewed natural-language catalogs and translated first-run guides.
- More sanitized fixtures for third-party application layouts and managed-Mac network policies.
- Improved trend summaries across multiple private baselines.

## Homebrew Core readiness

The tap is the supported Homebrew channel today. A Core submission becomes appropriate after the project demonstrates sustained external usage, stable releases, responsive maintenance, and no tap-specific dependency. The [readiness checklist](docs/homebrew-core-readiness.md) records the current official and project-specific gates; mactriage will not manufacture adoption signals or open a premature formula request.

## Out of scope

mactriage will remain a diagnostic CLI, not a cleaner, antivirus, remote-management agent, background daemon, telemetry product, security bypass, or automatic system tuner.
